How to use each part

  • Learn (start of lesson, whole class): choose a level and press the big button one step at a time. Level 1: Pip says the sounds and pupils copy. Level 2: the letters light up and pupils say the sounds and blend aloud. Level 3: pupils blend the whole word alone. Pupils then point to a picture, and the teacher drags the picture they choose onto Pip's board.
  • Gallery (during the block lesson): after blending with the blocks, pupils find the matching picture. Words are hidden at first so the pictures do not give the answer away.
  • Quiz game (end of lesson): type the class list once. Choose a pupil, press New word, and let everyone blend silently during the countdown. The chosen pupil says the word and tells the teacher which picture to tap. A first wrong answer lets classmates help, which is recorded as "S".
  • Chant (closure): the chant has no audio, so the teacher leads and the class chants "Sound it, stretch it, say it!" for each word. Press Pause to stop at any point, then Play to continue from the same place. Start always begins again from the chorus.
  • Shortcut: in Learn mode, the space bar or right arrow key presses the big button.
